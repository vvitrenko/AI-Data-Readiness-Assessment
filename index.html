What each readiness level means

0–24

Very Early

Your data foundation is not yet ready to reliably support the AI use case.

Focus: Establish a reliable data foundation.
25–49

Developing

Some foundations exist, but important gaps could make AI unreliable or difficult to scale.

Focus: Fix important data, governance, and access gaps.
50–69

Moderate

A workable foundation exists, but several capabilities need to mature before confident deployment and scale.

Focus: Strengthen and standardize capabilities.
70–84

Strong

Most key data capabilities are in place; remaining gaps are primarily around consistency, automation, and scale.

Focus: Operationalize and scale what works.
85–100

Advanced

A mature data foundation is well positioned to support the use case and broader AI adoption.

Focus: Maintain the foundation and expand responsibly.
