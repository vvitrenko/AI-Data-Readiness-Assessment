Is your data ready for AI?

This assessment is designed to help you understand whether your data is ready for an enterprise AI project. To get started, think of one specific process you are considering for AI. The assessment will walk you through four main domains your data should be great at for a successful AI project.

How to answer: Answer for the process you would actually automate first, not for the organization as a whole. Answer as things are today, not as they are meant to be.
